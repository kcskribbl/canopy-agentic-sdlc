# Canopy platform and first product: requirements

**Status:** Working requirements for review; not a new approval or a replacement for the [historical service requirements](../history.md). Source: [current product intent](INTENT.md). The identifiers below describe durable product requirements; a scenario's `AC-001` identifiers are local to its revision.

**Product journey:** An owner submits a request, clarifies its observable outcomes, reviews the plan, and authorizes execution on one candidate. QA and Security independently inspect that candidate. A reviewer can accept the resulting source checkpoint, decline it, or send the work back. The URL shortener is the first project on which this journey is exercised.

**Failure rules:** A stale approval cannot advance a new candidate. Replacing criteria removes old test mappings rather than asserting they cover a different requirement. A passing build alone cannot waive a security gate. A demo rollback restores managed source, not a database or deployed traffic.

**Validation limits and open decisions:** An operator must still review whether supplied or proposed criteria are sufficient and whether tests cover their meaning. The planner does not infer a complete runtime architecture. Real reviewer identity, hardened execution isolation and live infrastructure qualification remain separate decisions. For actual outcomes rather than requirements, read the [engineering summary](../../FINAL_ENGINEERING_SUMMARY.md).
